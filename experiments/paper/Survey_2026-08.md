# Systematic sweep — VLA efficiency, 2025–2026

*Run because `RelatedWork.md` §2.6 warns that "no competitor exists" is a
negative claim that needs re-checking before submission. It was right to warn:
the field moved, and **two of our claims have to change.***

**Evidence level, stated up front.** arXiv, OpenReview, Semantic Scholar and
HuggingFace are blocked by this environment's egress policy, so this sweep is
built from **search results and abstracts, not full texts**. Everything below
is marked with how it was established. The three papers marked ⚠ need PDFs
before Related Work is written.

---

## 1. The headline: our "no competitor" claim is now too strong

§2.6 says no prior paper crosses a backbone axis and a benchmark axis. Two
papers found in this sweep appear to.

### ⚠ SpecPrune-VLA (arXiv 2509.05614) — the one that matters

Reported in the search snippets:

- **Training-free.** *"SpecPrune-VLA is a training-free acceleration
  framework, meaning it does not require retraining… a training-free, two-level
  pruning method with heuristic control."*
- **Two backbones**: OpenVLA-OFT (VLM-based) and DB-OFT (diffusion-based) —
  *"OpenVLA-OFT and DB-OFT are selected as target models."*
- **Two benchmarks**: LIBERO (four suites) **and** SimplerEnv — and on
  SimplerEnv it uses **the same four WidowX-Bridge tasks we do** (spoon on
  towel, carrot on plate, stack cube, eggplant in basket), plus real-world
  tasks.
- The SimplerEnv result is reported *"compared to baseline models
  **OpenVLA-OFT and DB-OFT**"*, which reads as both backbones on both
  benchmarks.

If that reading holds, **the crossing cell is filled** and our novelty
sentence has to narrow. It also combines the two axes I recommended adding
(token pruning + self-speculative), on our exact Bridge task set.

**This is the single most important thing to verify before writing Related
Work.** Get the PDF and check one thing: is there a table with both backbones
on *both* benchmarks, or is SimplerEnv OpenVLA-OFT only, the way VLA-Pruner's
SIMPLER table is?

### ⚠ VLA-IAP (arXiv 2603.22991)

*"hardware efficiency analysis … across different VLA backbones and task
suites (CALVIN and LIBERO)."* Training-free visual token pruning. Same
question, same fix: get the PDF.

### What survives either way

Our claim has two halves, and only the first is threatened:

| our claim | status |
|---|---|
| nobody crosses both axes | ⚠ **probably false now** — SpecPrune-VLA, possibly VLA-IAP |
| **nobody pairs episodes and tests whether the sign holds** | still true as far as this sweep shows — every paper found reports mean success rates and speedups |

So the defensible novelty moves from *coverage* to *method*: others now run
several backbones and benchmarks; **what nobody does is match initial states,
count only the flipped episodes, and ask whether the direction survives.**
That is a narrower claim and a better one, because it is about what the
measurement can support rather than about how many cells were filled.

## 2. Two adjacent papers that change the framing of contribution 4

Our fourth contribution is "a measurement procedure for this class of claim."
Two 2026 papers occupy nearby ground and must be cited and distinguished.

| paper | what it does | how ours differs |
|---|---|---|
| ⚠ **vla-eval: A Unified Evaluation Harness for VLA Models** (OpenReview) | *"models and benchmarks integrate once and evaluate against everything, reproducing published scores across six codebases and three benchmarks"* | an **infrastructure** contribution — making evaluations runnable and comparable. Ours is a **statistical** one: pairing, determinism, a correction family. Complementary, but the contribution sentence must say which we mean |
| **StarVLA** (arXiv 2604.05014) | states that *"most existing VLA methods are evaluated on limited environments with substantially different preprocessing pipelines, policy interfaces, and evaluation protocols"* | **that is our problem statement, already in print.** We should cite it as agreeing with us rather than let a reviewer find it first |

**StarVLA is not a threat — it is support.** A published paper saying the
field's evaluation protocols are inconsistent makes our motivation someone
else's observation too. But our Introduction currently reads as if we are the
first to notice, and that has to change.

## 4. The training-free landscape, as far as abstracts show

Everything below claims training-free in its title or abstract. **None of these
is verified from a PDF** — and the MoLe-VLA lesson is that abstracts are not
evidence. They are listed so the sweep is on record, not so they can be cited.

| paper | arXiv | axis | claims training-free |
|---|---|---|---|
| SpecPrune-VLA | 2509.05614 | token pruning + self-speculative | yes ⚠ |
| Token Expand-Merge (TEAM-VLA) | 2512.09927 | token compression | yes ⚠ |
| VLA-InfoEntropy | 2604.05323 | entropy-gated dynamic inference | yes ⚠ |
| VLA-IAP | 2603.22991 | token pruning | yes ⚠ |
| DepthCache | 2603.10469 | depth-guided token merging | yes ⚠ |
| SAFE-Pruner | 2605.29662 | future-aware token pruning | yes ⚠ |
| SP-VLA | 2506.12723 | model scheduling + token pruning | yes ⚠ — OpenVLA-7B, LIBERO only |
| Environment-Aware Adaptive Pruning | 2602.00780 | token pruning | yes ⚠ |
| Action-aware Dynamic Pruning | 2509.22093 | token pruning | yes ⚠ |
| TTF-VLA | 2508.19257 | temporal token fusion | yes ⚠ |

**What this list means for us.** The token-pruning axis is *crowded* — ten-plus
papers in eighteen months. That is an argument for **not** adding it as a
fourth axis to compete on, and for using FastV instead as the *object* of
measurement: an established method that two independent groups already report
differently on VLM versus VLA. Our value is not another pruner; it is asking
whether any of them holds its sign.

## 6. Is Related Work ready to write?

**§2.2–2.5 yes, §2.6 no.**

The three axis sections and the reporting-practice section stand on material
that is fully verified from source. §2.6 — the "where we sit" section — is
exactly the part this sweep undermined, and it is the part a reviewer checks
hardest, because it is where the novelty claim lives.

**The one blocking item is SpecPrune-VLA's PDF.** If its crossing cell is
filled, we rewrite the novelty claim around pairing rather than coverage — which
is a better claim anyway, and one we can defend absolutely. If it is empty like
VLA-Pruner's, we keep the current claim with two more rows in the table.

Either way the paper is fine. But writing §2.6 before knowing which is
writing it twice.
