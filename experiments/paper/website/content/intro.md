Supplementary material for the paper: exact trick settings, results for every setting rather than the best one per trick, per-task breakdowns, the statistics behind the paired tests, qualitative rollouts and discussion points. Every number is computed from the same per-episode records as the paper.

**The paper in brief.** Vision-language-action (VLA) policies are strong but slow, and training-free efficiency methods are usually evaluated on one backbone and one benchmark. We evaluate five representative tricks, acting on what the policy sees (visual foveation), when it is called (action repeat, guarded action reuse) and how much it computes per call (depth pruning, temporal feature fusion), under one matched protocol on seven released VLA backbones and three simulated environments (SimplerEnv WidowX, SimplerEnv Google Robot "Fractal", LIBERO). Every configuration replays the same task instances and initial states as the unmodified policy: 22 backbone and environment pairs, 14 configurations each, 47,600 paired episodes.
