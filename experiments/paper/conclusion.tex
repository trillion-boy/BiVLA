\section{Conclusion, Limitation and Future Works}

We evaluated five training-free interventions on seven released VLA
policies and three environments under one matched protocol, 47,600
episodes in all, with every intervened episode paired to its dense control.
No intervention is a reliable gain. Significant changes are drops by more
than twelve to one, the few gains are of the size and number that chance
allows, and the sign of an intervention is a joint property of the rule,
the backbone and the environment, so a claim made on one combination does
not transfer to another without a matched test on the target. Lower
computation implies neither lower latency nor preserved success, and the
one family that never harms success, guarded reuse, is harmless because its
gates rarely fire. The per-episode record with per-step gate counts is what
separates a real effect from a silent gate and a task-specific loss from a
global one, and we recommend that any gated intervention report how often
its gate fired.

\textbf{Limitations.} Each cell is one run, so the paired test speaks to
the same initial states under two policies and not to other initial states.
CronusVLA does not reproduce its rollout under a fixed seed, so its cells
carry a run-to-run floor and its conclusions are weaker than the other
six. Latency is read only within one backbone, environment, software
stack and GPU class, so SmolVLA's guarded reuse and temporal fusion report
success alone, and peak memory was not measured. One cell, OpenVLA Long
under conservative-adaptive fusion, is reported without a paired test. All
runs are in simulation, and the WidowX and Fractal checkpoints run under
SimplerEnv's visual matching protocol rather than on the robot.

\textbf{Future work.} A matched protocol with several seeds per cell would
turn the single-run limit into a measured variance. Real-robot replication
of the sign flips reported in Section~IV-B would test whether they survive
the simulator. The reuse mask of temporal fusion is exposed to the cache
path but not yet executed under the optimized attention backend, and
measuring that integration is the natural next step. Finally, the paired
per-episode records and gate counts of every run make it possible to test
a new intervention against the same episodes under the same test.
