\section{Conclusion, Limitation and Future Works}

We evaluated five training-free interventions on seven released VLA
policies and three environments under one matched protocol, 47,600 paired
episodes in all. No intervention is a reliable gain. Significant changes
are drops by more than twelve to one, the few gains are of the size and
number that chance allows, and the sign of an intervention is a joint
property of the rule, the backbone and the environment, so a claim made on
one combination does not transfer to another without a matched test on the
target. Lower computation implies neither lower latency nor preserved
success, and the one family that never harms success, guarded reuse, is
harmless because its gates rarely fire, which only the per-episode record
with per-step gate counts can show.

\textbf{Limitations and future work.} Each cell is one run, so the paired
test speaks to the same initial states under two policies and not to other
initial states, and CronusVLA does not reproduce its rollout under a fixed
seed. Latency is read only within one backbone, environment, software stack
and GPU class, peak memory was not measured, and all runs are in
simulation. Several seeds per cell, real-robot replication of the sign
flips of Section~IV-B, and a measured integration of the fusion reuse mask
with the cache path are the next steps, and the paired records and gate
counts of every run allow a new intervention to be tested against the same
episodes.
