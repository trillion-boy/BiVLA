\section{Bag of Tricks}

A VLA policy $\pi_\theta$ maps an image and an instruction to an action $a_t$
through a visual encoder and a stack of decoder blocks. The reference is the
released policy run unchanged under fused attention. Every intervention below
leaves $\theta$ frozen and changes one part of inference, at settings listed in
Section~IV-A. The first two are negative controls and the last three are the
candidates. Related Work gives the lineage of each. Here we state only what
runs.

\subsection{Foveation}

Fixed foveation keeps a sharp center and suppresses the periphery at a keep
fraction $\rho$. The log-polar variant warps the frame about its center, keeps
a fraction $\rho$ of the samples, and inverts the warp. The blur variant keeps
a central disc and blends increasingly blurred copies outside it. Output
resolution and visual token count are unchanged, so foveation tests
perceptual simplification alone.

\subsection{Action Repeat}

Unconditional action repeat executes each dense action for $k$ consecutive
steps and queries the policy on none of the repeated steps. It divides model
calls per environment step by $k$ and lengthens by $k$ the interval the robot
acts on without fresh feedback, with no gate. For a backbone that emits action
chunks, one call then covers $k$ times the chunk length.

\subsection{Depth Pruning}

Calibrated depth removal scores each decoder block by ShortGPT block
influence, one minus the mean cosine between its input and output hidden
states~\cite{shortgpt}, on a calibration split disjoint from the test
episodes. The selector protects the first quarter of the stack and the final
block, forbids removing adjacent blocks, and fixes the removed set before
testing. Removed blocks are excised and the generation cache reindexed, so
this is removal rather than an identity block. The budget, how many blocks
are removed, is the swept quantity.

\subsection{Guarded Reuse}

Guarded action reuse repeats the previous action only when a subsampled pixel
signature is stable at frame and patch scale, the two most recent dense actions
agree in direction and gripper state, and the commanded translation is above a
floor, which disables reuse in low-motion precision phases. Consecutive reuse
is capped, and any failed gate returns to dense inference, so every step still
sees a current image. The image and agreement thresholds and the cap are swept
together as one setting, strict, moderate, or aggressive, from the tightest to
the loosest, with the translation floor fixed.

\subsection{Temporal Fusion}

Interaction-aware temporal fusion scores each visual patch by motion, image
entropy, and optional text-to-vision relevance, protects patches high in any of
these or adjacent to one, and marks at most a fixed fraction of the rest
reusable. A reusable patch takes the previous step's projected token, the
others are recomputed, and a dense keyframe every few steps bounds propagation.
Fusion changes which tokens enter the decoder, not how many, so it is an
accuracy candidate rather than a speed one. Three settings are evaluated.
Motion-entropy builds the mask from motion and entropy alone, task-aware adds
text-to-vision relevance, and conservative-adaptive keeps the motion-entropy
mask with a smaller reuse fraction, a shorter keyframe interval, and a forced
keyframe whenever any patch moves beyond a threshold.
