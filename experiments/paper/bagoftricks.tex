\section{Bag of Tricks}

A VLA policy $\pi_\theta$ maps an image and an instruction to an action $a_t$
through a visual encoder and a stack of decoder blocks. The reference is the
released policy run unchanged under fused attention. Every intervention below
leaves $\theta$ frozen and changes one part of inference, at settings listed in
Section~IV-A. The first two are controls and the last three are the candidates.
Related Work gives the lineage of each. Here we state only what runs.

\subsection{Foveation}

Fixed foveation keeps a central disc sharp, covering a fraction $\rho$ of the
image area, and blends increasingly blurred copies of the frame outside it.
Output resolution and visual token count are unchanged, so foveation tests
perceptual simplification alone.

\subsection{Action Repeat}

Unconditional action repeat executes each dense action for $k$ consecutive
steps and queries the policy on none of the repeated steps. It divides model
calls per environment step by $k$ and multiplies by $k$ the interval the robot
acts on without fresh feedback, with no gate. Where the harness executes a full
action chunk per call, one call then covers $k$ times the chunk length.

\subsection{Depth Pruning}

Calibrated depth pruning scores each decoder layer by ShortGPT Block Influence,
one minus the mean cosine between its input and output hidden
states~\cite{shortgpt}, on a calibration split disjoint from the test episodes.
The selector protects the first quarter of the stack and the final layer,
forbids removing adjacent layers, and fixes the removed set before testing.
Removed layers are excised and the generation cache reindexed, so this is
removal rather than an identity layer. The budget, how many layers are removed,
is the swept quantity.

\subsection{Guarded Reuse}

Guarded action reuse repeats the previous action only when a subsampled pixel
signature is stable at frame and patch scale, the two most recent dense actions
agree in direction and gripper state, and the commanded translation is above a
floor, which disables reuse in low-motion precision phases. Consecutive reuse
is capped, and any failed gate returns to dense inference, so the gates read a
current image at every step and only the model call is skipped. The image and
agreement thresholds and the cap are swept together as one setting, strict,
moderate, or aggressive, from the tightest to the loosest, with the translation
floor fixed.

\subsection{Temporal Fusion}

Temporal fusion scores each visual patch by motion, image entropy, and optional
text-to-vision relevance, protects patches high in any of these or adjacent to
one, and marks at most a fixed fraction of the rest reusable. A reusable patch
keeps the token that entered the decoder at the previous step, the others take
the fresh token, and a dense keyframe every few steps bounds propagation.
Fusion changes which tokens enter the decoder, not how many, so it is an
accuracy candidate rather than a speed one. Three settings are evaluated.
Motion-entropy builds the mask from motion and entropy alone, task-aware adds
text-to-vision relevance, and conservative-adaptive keeps the motion-entropy
mask with a smaller reuse fraction, a shorter keyframe interval, and a forced
keyframe whenever any patch moves beyond a threshold.
