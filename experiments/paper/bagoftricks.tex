\section{Bag of Tricks}

A VLA policy $\pi_\theta$ maps the observation $o_t$ at step $t$ and an
instruction to an action $a_t$, or to a chunk of $m$ actions, through a visual
encoder, a stack of decoder layers, and an action head. The reference, called
dense below and Original policy in the tables, is the released policy run
unchanged under fused attention. Every intervention below leaves $\theta$
frozen and changes one part of inference, at settings listed in Section~IV-A.
The first two are controls and the last three are the candidates. Related Work
gives the lineage of each. Here we state only what runs.

\subsection{Foveation}

Fixed foveation keeps sharp a central disc covering a fraction $\rho$ of the
image area, the keep ratio, and blends increasingly blurred copies of the frame
outside it. Output resolution and visual token count are unchanged, so
foveation tests perceptual simplification alone.

\subsection{Action Repeat}

Action repeat executes each dense action for $k$ consecutive steps and skips
the model call on the repeated steps. It divides model calls per environment
step by $k$ and multiplies by $k$ the interval over which the robot acts
without fresh feedback, with no gate. Where the harness executes a full action
chunk per call, one call then covers $k$ times the chunk length.

\subsection{Depth Pruning}

Calibrated depth pruning scores each decoder layer by ShortGPT Block Influence,
one minus the mean cosine between its input and output hidden
states~\cite{shortgpt}, on a calibration split disjoint from the test episodes.
The selector protects a leading fraction of the stack and the final layer,
forbids removing adjacent layers, and fixes the removed set before testing.
Removed layers are excised and the generation cache reindexed, so this is
removal rather than an identity layer. The budget, how many layers are removed,
is the swept quantity.

\subsection{Guarded Reuse}

Guarded action reuse repeats the previous action only when a subsampled pixel
signature is stable at whole-frame and local scale, the two most recent dense
actions agree in direction and gripper state, and the commanded translation is
above a floor, so reuse never fires while the commanded motion is small.
Consecutive reuse is capped, and any failed gate returns to dense inference.
The gates read a current image at every step, so only the model call is
skipped. The image and agreement thresholds and the cap are swept together as
one setting, strict, moderate, or aggressive, from the tightest to the loosest,
with the translation floor fixed.

\subsection{Temporal Fusion}

Temporal fusion scores each visual patch by motion, image entropy, and optional
text-to-vision attention, protects patches high in any of these or adjacent to
one, and marks reusable at most a fixed fraction of all patches, drawn from the
rest. A reusable patch keeps the token that entered the decoder at the previous
step while the others take the fresh token, and a dense keyframe every few
steps bounds propagation. Fusion changes which tokens enter the decoder, not
how many, so it is an accuracy candidate rather than a speed one. Three
settings are evaluated. Motion-entropy builds the reusable set from motion and
entropy alone, task-aware adds text-to-vision attention, and
conservative-adaptive keeps the motion-entropy selection with a smaller reuse
fraction, a shorter keyframe interval, and a forced keyframe whenever any patch
moves beyond a second, higher motion threshold.
