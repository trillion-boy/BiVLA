--- slide 1
Bag of Tricks for Training-Free Vision-Language-Action Models
Motivation
  Efficient VLA inference without retraining
The control loop of a frozen VLA policy (Fig. 2 of the paper). Green, orange, purple, and blue boxes mark where the five tricks enter; Redundancy Control is action repeat.
[1] 
VLA inference is slow. 
Tens to hundreds of milliseconds per step (60 to 450 ms in our runs), which limits how often the policy can observe, decide, and correct.
[2] 
Gains are reported in isolation. 
Training-free tricks are typically evaluated for a specific trick, backbone, benchmark, and implementation, so transfer across models is unknown.
Do the gains transfer across VLA models and environments, 
or are they configuration-dependent?

Trick 1  Visual foveation
What to see
Sharp central disc, progressively blurred periphery
Keep ratio 20% or 50%; token count unchanged, no latency saving
Tests whether high-frequency detail is needed only near the objects and the gripper
Original
Foveation 20%
CogACT on WidowX, Spoon on towel, same episode, 2× speed. Green border = success, red = failure.
--- slide 3
Trick 2  Action repeat
When to act
Hold each predicted action for k = 2 or 4 steps, about one call per k steps
Cuts policy calls by about k, but lengthens the open-loop interval
Fixed reduction, no state check: a speed-oriented baseline
Original
Action repeat 2
CogACT on WidowX, Carrot on plate, same episode, 2× speed. Green border = success, red = failure.
--- slide 4
Trick 3  Depth pruning
How much to compute
Score each decoder block by Block Influence
Remove 1, 2, or 4 low-influence blocks; none adjacent, early blocks and the final block kept
Fixed once from calibration data, not adapted at test time
Original
Depth pruning 2
CogACT on WidowX, Carrot on plate, same episode, 2× speed. Green border = success, red = failure.
--- slide 5
Trick 4  Guarded action reuse
When to act
Reuse the previous action only when all gates pass: small global and local image change, recent actions agree, translation not near zero, gripper unchanged, reuse count below a cap
Presets: strict, moderate, aggressive
Original
Reuse aggressive
CogACT on WidowX, Stack cube, same episode, 2× speed. Green border = success, red = failure.
--- slide 6
Trick 5  Temporal fusion
How much to compute
Reuse visual tokens of stable patches from the previous call
Recompute patches with motion, local structure, language attention, or in the interaction region
Presets: motion-entropy, task-aware, conservative-adaptive
Top: fusion mask. Bottom: VLA-Cache criterion.
Original
Fusion motion
CogACT on WidowX, Spoon on towel, same episode, 2× speed. Green border = success, red = failure.

--- slide 19
Takeaways
Guarded reuse
Most reliable. Preserves success, modest speedup when the gates open.
Action repeat
Largest speedup, but the risk of long open-loop execution.
Depth pruning
Decoder-level savings, benefit depends on the backbone.
Foveation · Temporal fusion
Test how much the visual representation can change while the policy stays stable.
VLA efficiency is policy- and environment-dependent. Evaluate every trick under the target backbone and environment before applying it.
